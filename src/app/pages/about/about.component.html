Our Story

We make less,
but heavier.

Monk Hagen is a streetwear house built on discipline, weight and restraint.

Monk Hagen started with a simple frustration — fast fashion that fell apart after a season. We set out to build the opposite: heavyweight pieces made in limited runs, designed to be worn, beaten and worn again.

Every drop is intentional. We don't restock. When a piece is gone, it makes room for the next idea. That constraint keeps us honest and keeps our collection sharp.
