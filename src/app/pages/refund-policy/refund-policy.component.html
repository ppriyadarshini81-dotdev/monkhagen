Refund, Return & Exchange Policy

Effective Date: October 12, 2026

Monk Hagen (“Monk Hagen,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to ensuring customer satisfaction with every purchase made through the Monk Hagen website (the “Website”). This Refund, Return & Exchange Policy (“Policy”) sets out the terms and conditions applicable to returns, exchanges, refunds, and related matters in respect of orders placed on the Website. Customers are encouraged to read this Policy carefully before placing an order.

1. Return Window

1.1 Customers may request a return of an eligible product within seven (7) days from the date of delivery of the relevant order (the “Return Window”).

1.2 Damaged, defective or incorrect products are not governed by this Return Window and are instead subject to the separate reporting timeline set out in Section 4.

1.3 To initiate a return, customers must raise a return request through the Website against the relevant order, within the Return Window.

1.4 Return requests raised after expiry of the Return Window will not be accepted.

1.5 A return shipping fee applies to returns approved under this Section, as set out in Section 5 (Refund Method).

2. Return Eligibility

2.1 To qualify for a return, the product must satisfy all of the following conditions:

  • Unworn, unused and unwashed;
  • Free from stains, marks, perfume, deodorant or any other signs of use;
  • Accompanied by all original tags and labels, intact and attached;
  • Returned in its original packaging;
  • In the same condition as at the time of delivery; and
  • Free from any alteration, damage or modification by the customer.

2.2 Products that do not satisfy the above conditions may be rejected upon quality inspection and returned to the customer.

3. Return Process

3.1 Once a return request is raised through the Website, the product will be collected from the customer by our delivery partner and transported to our warehouse.

3.2 Returned products are subject to inspection upon receipt at our facility.

3.3 Monk Hagen reserves the right, in its sole discretion, to approve or reject a return based on whether the product satisfies the eligibility conditions set out in Section 2.

3.4 Where a return is rejected, the product will not be refunded and will be returned to the customer in its original, unaltered condition.

3.5 Where a return is approved, the applicable refund will be processed in accordance with Section 5 (Refund Method).

3.6 Customers are requested to allow reasonable processing time following receipt of the returned product.

4. Reporting and Returning Defective, Damaged or Incorrect Products

4.1 Customers must report a damaged, defective or incorrect product within forty-eight (48) hours of the date and time of delivery, by uploading photographs of the reported defect through the Website.

4.2 We may additionally request further photographs or video evidence of the product to assist in assessing the reported issue.

4.3 Monk Hagen will review the submitted evidence and reserves the right to reject the report where, in its assessment, the evidence does not establish a genuine defect, damage or discrepancy.

4.4 Where a report is approved, the customer may choose between a replacement of the product or a return with refund, and the selected resolution will be scheduled accordingly.

4.5 No shipping or handling fee will be deducted in respect of an approved claim under this Section.

4.6 Where a refund is approved under this Section, it will be processed in accordance with Section 5 (Refund Method), within three (3) business days of approval.

4.7 Issues falling within the exclusions set out in Section 6 will not be treated as a defect for the purposes of this Section.

5. Refund Method

5.1 Approved refunds will be processed to the original payment method used to place the order.

5.2 Where the relevant order was placed on a Cash on Delivery (COD) basis, the customer must provide valid bank account details to enable processing of the refund. The refund cannot be processed without these details.

5.3 For approved returns under Sections 1 to 3 (standard returns), a return shipping and handling fee of ₹150 will be deducted from the refund amount.

5.4 No shipping or handling fee will be deducted from refunds relating to approved claims under Section 4 (defective, damaged or incorrect products).

5.5 Shipping charges paid on the original order, if any, are non-refundable in all cases.

5.6 Refunds for standard returns will be processed within a reasonable time following approval of the return in accordance with Section 3.

6. Exclusions from Manufacturing Defects

6.1 Owing to the nature of garments and the manufacturing process, minor variations may occur and will not, in general, be regarded as manufacturing defects, including:

  • Minor variations in stitching;
  • Slight fabric creases or wrinkles;
  • Minor loose threads that do not affect the construction or functionality of the garment;
  • Minor variations in print appearance resulting from the printing process; and
  • Normal characteristics of the fabric or garment resulting from manufacturing, washing or finishing processes.

6.2 Customers who believe a product has a genuine manufacturing defect are encouraged to contact us, and each report will be assessed fairly.

7. Sale & Discounted Products

7.1 Products purchased during a sale, clearance or promotional offer are not eligible for return or exchange, except where the product received is damaged, defective or incorrect.

7.2 Any additional terms applicable to a specific promotional offer shall apply in conjunction with this Policy.

8. Exchanges

8.1 Size Exchange: Customers may request one (1) size exchange per order, within seven (7) days of delivery, at no additional shipping cost, subject to availability of the requested size.

8.2 Where a size exchange is requested, the product must satisfy all applicable return eligibility conditions set out in Section 2.

8.3 Customers are advised to review the size chart provided on the product page before placing an order.

8.4 Availability of the requested size is subject to stock availability.

9. Order Cancellation

9.1 Orders cannot be cancelled or modified once shipping has commenced.

9.2 Cancellation requests should be submitted as soon as possible after the order is placed; we will endeavour to accommodate such requests where the order has not yet entered shipping.

10. Shipping Address Changes

10.1 The shipping address provided at the time of placing an order cannot be changed once the order has been placed.

10.2 Customers are responsible for ensuring the accuracy of the shipping address, PIN code and mobile number at the time of purchase.

11. Delivery Timelines

11.1 We aim to deliver orders within India within approximately two (2) to four (4) business days, depending on the destination and courier service.

11.2 Delivery timelines are estimates and may be affected by factors beyond our control, including courier delays, weather conditions, operational disruptions, public holidays or inaccurate address information.

12. Failed Delivery Attempts

12.1 Our delivery partners may make multiple attempts to deliver an order. Customers are requested to ensure availability to receive the package and that the mobile number provided remains reachable.

12.2 Where an order is returned to us due to repeated failed delivery attempts, an incorrect or incomplete address, or refusal to accept delivery, additional shipping charges may apply should the customer request re-shipment.

13. Contact Us

Customers may contact us at officialmail.monkhagen@gmail.com, quoting the order number for prompt assistance.

14. General

14.1 By placing an order on the Website, customers acknowledge that they have read and understood this Policy.

14.2 Monk Hagen reserves the right to reject a return, exchange or defective-product claim where the product or claim does not meet the requirements set out herein, or where there is evidence of use, damage, alteration or misuse.

14.3 We are committed to handling every return, exchange and refund request fairly and transparently.

15. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

15.1 This Policy shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India.

15.2 Subject to applicable law, any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Policy, or any return, exchange or refund made pursuant to it, shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts at Ludhiana, Punjab.
