Refund, Return & Exchange Policy

Effective Date: August 23, 2026

Monk Hagen ("Monk Hagen," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to ensuring customer satisfaction with every purchase made through the Monk Hagen website (the "Website"). This Policy sets out the terms and conditions applicable to returns, exchanges, refunds, and related matters. Customers are encouraged to read this Policy carefully before placing an order.

1. Return Window

1.1 Customers may request a return within seven (7) days from the date of delivery of the relevant order (the "Return Window").

1.2 To initiate a return, contact our customer support team within the Return Window at officialmail.monkhagen@gmail.com, quoting the order number and reason for the return.

1.3 Requests received after expiry of the Return Window will not be eligible for return.

2. Return Eligibility

2.1 To qualify for a return, the product must satisfy all of the following conditions:

  • Unworn, unused and unwashed;
  • Free from stains, marks, perfume, deodorant or any other signs of use;
  • Accompanied by all original tags and labels, intact and attached;
  • Returned in its original packaging;
  • In the same condition as at the time of delivery; and
  • Free from any alteration, damage or modification by the customer.

2.2 Products that do not satisfy the above conditions may be rejected upon quality inspection and returned to the customer.

3. Return Process

3.1 Upon receipt of a return request, our team will review the request and provide the applicable return instructions.

3.2 Returned products are subject to a quality inspection upon receipt.

3.3 On successful completion of the quality inspection, the return will be approved and the applicable refund will be processed in accordance with the customer's selected refund method, as set out in Section 4.

3.4 Customers are requested to allow reasonable processing time following receipt of the returned product.

4. Refund Method

4.1 For eligible returns, customers may choose to receive the refund in either of the following ways:

  • Store Credit, redeemable towards a future purchase on the Website; or
  • A refund to the original bank account or payment method used to place the order.

4.2 Where a customer opts for Store Credit, the full value of the eligible amount will be credited without any deduction.

4.3 Where a customer opts for a refund to the original bank account or payment method, a return shipping and handling fee of ₹100 will be deducted from the refund amount.

4.4 Shipping charges paid on the original order, if any, are non-refundable under either refund option.

4.5 The selected refund will be processed upon receipt of the returned product and successful completion of the quality inspection.

5. Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Products

5.1 Customers who receive a product that is damaged, defective or incorrect should notify us as soon as possible following delivery.

5.2 We may request photographic or video evidence of the product to assess the reported issue.

5.3 Upon return of the product, our team will inspect it to determine whether the issue constitutes a manufacturing defect, damage in transit, or an incorrect item dispatched by Monk Hagen.

5.4 Where the issue is verified, we will provide an appropriate resolution, which may include:

  • Replacement of the product; or
  • A refund of the product amount, as applicable.

5.5 Where a refund is approved, it will be processed to the original payment method within three (3) business days of completion of the quality inspection.

6. Exclusions from Manufacturing Defects

6.1 Owing to the nature of garments and the manufacturing process, minor variations may occur and will not, in general, be regarded as manufacturing defects, including:

  • Minor variations in stitching;
  • Slight fabric creases or wrinkles;
  • Minor loose threads that do not affect the construction or functionality of the garment;
  • Minor variations in print appearance resulting from the printing process; and
  • Normal characteristics of the fabric resulting from manufacturing, washing or finishing processes.

6.2 Customers who believe a product has a genuine manufacturing defect are encouraged to contact us, and each report will be assessed fairly.

7. Sale & Discounted Products

7.1 Products purchased during a sale, clearance or promotional offer are not eligible for return or exchange, except where the product received is damaged, defective or incorrect.

7.2 Any additional terms applicable to a specific promotional offer shall apply in conjunction with this Policy.

8. Exchanges

8.1 Exchanges are offered only where specifically permitted for the relevant product or order.

8.2 Where an exchange is available, the product must satisfy all applicable return eligibility conditions set out in Section 2.

8.3 Customers are advised to review the size chart provided on the product page before placing an order.

8.4 Availability of the requested replacement product or size is subject to stock availability.

9. Order Cancellation

9.1 Orders are processed promptly upon being placed and cannot be cancelled or modified once processing or shipping has commenced.

9.2 Cancellation requests should be submitted as soon as possible after the order is placed; we will endeavour to accommodate such requests where the order has not yet entered processing.

9.3 Cancellation is not possible once an order has been shipped.

10. Shipping Address Changes

10.1 Requests to change a shipping address should be submitted as soon as possible after the order is placed.

10.2 Address changes can only be accommodated before the order has entered processing or shipping.

10.3 Once an order has been shipped, we may be unable to modify the delivery address.

10.4 Customers are responsible for ensuring the accuracy of the shipping address, PIN code and mobile number at the time of purchase.

11. Delivery Timelines

11.1 We aim to deliver orders within India within approximately two (2) to four (4) business days, depending on the destination and courier service.

11.2 Delivery timelines are estimates and may be affected by factors beyond our control, including courier delays, weather conditions, operational disruptions, public holidays or inaccurate address information.

12. Failed Delivery Attempts

12.1 Our delivery partners may make multiple attempts to deliver an order. Customers are requested to ensure availability to receive the package and that the mobile number provided remains reachable.

12.2 Where an order is returned to us due to repeated failed delivery attempts, an incorrect or incomplete address, or refusal to accept delivery, additional shipping charges may apply should the customer request re-shipment.

13. Contact Us

For queries relating to returns, exchanges, refunds or products, please contact our customer support team at officialmail.monkhagen@gmail.com. Customers are requested to quote their order number in the subject line to enable prompt assistance.

14. General

14.1 By placing an order on the Website, customers acknowledge that they have read and understood this Policy.

14.2 Monk Hagen reserves the right to reject a return or exchange where the product does not meet the eligibility requirements set out herein, or where there is evidence of use, damage, alteration or misuse.

14.3 We are committed to handling every return and refund request fairly and transparently.
